How it works

  1. Clean the text. Lowercase it, strip HTML, and replace links, addresses and numbers with markers.
  2. Turn words into numbers. TF-IDF scores how important each word and word pair is.
  3. Classify. A Multinomial Naive Bayes model, trained on the SpamAssassin public email corpus, estimates the probability of spam.

Limitation: the training emails date from 2002–2005, so very new spam styles may be missed.
